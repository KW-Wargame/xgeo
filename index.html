02

Features

Computed, not scripted. Filmed in the game.

01

Real orbits

Every move is solved, not animated: Lambert's problem under the Earth's flattened gravity, the Sun and the Moon. The satellites start on their real orbits, and fuel trades against time.

Lambert transfers · J2, Sun and Moon · SGP4 from real element sets

02

Telescopes, sunlight and shadow

A telescope sees a satellite only by the sunlight it reflects or the heat it gives off, worked out as signal-to-noise for every look. In the Earth's shadow it sees nothing.

Planck's law in band · Real sunlight and shadow

03

Signals intelligence

Hear the inspector's reports to its ground stations, fix them by direction finding, and identify each emitter by what its signal measures.

Friis link budgets · Direction finding · Modulation

04

Tracking, and presence

Real ground radars see the inspector only on their passes; your own telescope holds it in between. Every track carries its uncertainty. And you win by being there: park close and it gives way, push too hard and tension climbs.

Kalman tracking · Real radar sites · Escalation, not shooting

05

Forecasts you can plan by

Before you move, see when you will be seen: every sensor's look at you over the coming orbit, in sunlight and in shadow.

Every sensor, every look, computed ahead

06

Platforms and equipment, from specs

Each platform is built from its equipment: dish sizes, power, thrust. Performance is computed from them, and every value cites its source or says it is a design value. The satellites are real, with their public specs.

Specs in, performance out · Every number sourced

03

xGEO Core

The engine under the game. Free and open source.

Core running the game's Close Pass scenario for 24 hours, no orders given, plotted straight from its output: where Cosmos 2612 really is, where Blue thinks it is, and the radar passes in between
  • OrbitsThe Earth's gravity with its flattening, the Sun and the Moon, integrated to a part in 10¹². Burns are planned, then refined under full gravity.
  • SensorsRadio links and antenna patterns from ITU references, ground radar on the turning Earth, infrared and visible signatures.
  • TrackingAn unscented Kalman filter keeps each track from the fixes it gets, and says how sure it is.
  • Checked681 comparisons against JPL Horizons, scipy, astropy and published worked examples. All pass.
  • RepeatableOne seed gives the same run on any machine. Scenarios are JSON; results come out as JSON lines.
  1. Download1.6 MB. Needs Godot 4.7, free.
  2. RunOne command, JSON lines out.godot --headless --path core/godot --script res://cli/run.gd -- --scenario= --hours=24
  3. Make it yoursApache 2.0. Change your own copy; just don't call it xGEO.
